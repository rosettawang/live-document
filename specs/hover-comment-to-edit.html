Hover anywhere, say what's wrong

Status: done done · created Aug 5, 2026 · shipped & verified Aug 5, 2026 · priority 30

Rosetta, Aug 5, 2026: "allow for hover to add comment anywhere which then runs through claude and changes the content." Point at the thing that's wrong, say why in your own words, and it gets rewritten.

Why this is the better of the two AI surfaces, and should be the one people find first

The chat box makes you describe where before you can say what: "in the activity research section, the bit about the Blue Lagoon…". The hover comment carries the location for free — you are already pointing at it — so the whole message can be the actual thought: "too expensive". Three words that would take a sentence and a half in chat.

That is also why it is cheaper: the request only has to carry the commented block's section rather than the whole document.

  1. Hover a block (long-press on touch) → the handle strip appears at its top-right.
  2. Click 💬 → a popover anchored under the block, one textarea, placeholder examples that teach the range: "too expensive — find something cheaper", "add Ed and Ben's flights once known", "turn this into a table".
  3. Send → the popover closes, the block dims and says "Claude is rewriting this…", and the chat panel opens with your comment already logged.
  4. The rewritten block lands for everyone with a violet flash and Claude in its margin.

The block is the progress indicator

No spinner in a corner, no toast. The block you commented on is the thing that dims, and the label sits on it. That matters because the round trip is a few seconds and the page is long: a spinner somewhere else means scrolling to find out whether anything happened. It also means two people can have two comments in flight on two blocks and each sees their own.

The one failure this must not have: a comment that silently does nothing

Three ways it can end with no visible change — the model decided it was a question, the key is missing, the daily cap is spent. All three write a line into the chat log rather than just clearing the busy state. A dimmed block that un-dims with nothing to show for it is the interaction people stop trusting after one occurrence.

Cost, because this is the button people will press most

The comment path sends the outline plus only the commented block's section — roughly a tenth of what the chat path sends. Against the account's $28.22 credit balance (checked Aug 5, 2026) that is the difference between a few hundred comments and a few dozen. Metering is per person per day and shared with chat — see access-and-identity.
